Data contract:
- Inclusive Argentina intake-date cohort, current snapshot, default month-to-date.
  Not historical state at the end date. Collections and costs cover all recorded
  history of those orders, not payment/expense dates within the intake period.
- Assigned technician_id, never created_by. Current assignment is not historical
  attribution of all labor or collections. Unassigned has its own group.
- Acceptance uses the latest decision for the current immutable budget revision
  and a consistent current approval flag. Old/legacy accepted states alone do
  not count. Rate denominator is current accepted plus current rejected decisions.
- Actual native non-voided/non-mirror payments plus canonical repair_payments
  through repairs.repair_access_order_id. No invoice totals, invoice payment
  mirrors, is_paid amount inference, or duplicate native legacy projections.
- Charge basis: nonzero final_amount, current accepted immutable quote amount
  (evidenced zero retained), or unique linked historical final_price. Zero final
  defaults no longer hide a nonzero quote or imply an unpriced service is free.
  Unknown charge makes complete
  cohort debt NULL, with an explicitly partial known debt subtotal.
- Consumed parts cost = installed_quantity * unit_cost. Unconsumed purchases
  are not costs. Non-cancelled outsourcing uses actual_cost, not promised cost.
- Unknown unit/actual costs, unstructured part history, imported history without
  structured costs and unclassified REP expenses preserve full cost/margin NULL.
  Known cost subtotals remain visible, and explicit zero costs stay zero.
- Procurement thread 01a10dda-b0b6-7d20-8245-dbf95f51fe88 confirmed compatibility:
  expenses.part_request_id is a supplier payment, not another consumption cost.
  REP-only expenses lack an explicit outsourcing/cost classification; expose them
  without adding them twice and conservatively mark costs incomplete.
- Direct margin is charge basis minus recorded direct cost. Never net profit,
  distributable income, cash balance or labor/overhead-complete profitability.
- Warranty metrics count coverage/review flags, not warranty reentries/rework rate.
- Median/P90 = current intake age of undelivered, non-retired orders. Exclude
  future intake dates. No fabricated phase-wait measurement.
- Category counts use the intake cohort, but links open supported REP scope
  filters on complete current queues. UI explicitly says links do not preserve
  cohort dates because the existing REP page does not support intake-date filters.
- Dashboard reuses getWorkshopInbox and WorkshopInbox. Current operational
  queue is independent of selected dashboard financial dates. Missing RPC is
  visibly unavailable, not zero pending work. Technicians fetch no business RPC.
