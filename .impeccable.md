## Design Context

### Users
Chetech is used daily by the owner and staff of a local technology/service shop. The interface needs to work quickly at the counter for sales, repairs, expenses, invoicing, stock checks, and cash control.

### Brand Personality
Precise, technical, sober. The brand should feel like professional stationery from a repair lab: reliable, organized, sharp, and practical rather than decorative.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a grayscale-first visual system inspired by the Chetech logo and stationery. Prefer graphite, warm off-white paper tones, soft gray borders, and restrained contrast. Avoid green as a dominant panel color; use color only for destructive/error states or rare semantic alerts.

### Design Principles
- The UI should feel like an operational document system, not a generic SaaS dashboard.
- Black, graphite, gray, and paper-white are the main palette.
- Logos and brand typography should appear where they improve trust and orientation.
- Forms must stay fast, legible, and usable on desktop and mobile.
- Visual changes should never make daily workflows slower.
